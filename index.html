FAQ

Common questions

Do the vehicles come with a driver?

Yes. Every booking includes a verified, experienced driver unless you've arranged a self-drive contract with us.

How is the price calculated?

Based on distance, vehicle type and load. You receive one fixed quote before confirming.

Can I book for multiple days or a monthly contract?

Absolutely. Mention it in the contact form and we'll prepare a custom plan.

Is my cargo insured?

Basic transit cover is available on request, and higher-value goods can be insured separately.

How quickly can a vehicle arrive?

In major cities, often within a few hours. For remote routes we recommend booking a day ahead.
